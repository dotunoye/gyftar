We do not sell gifts.
We craft legacies.

Every Gyftar box is a deliberate act of care. We spend months sourcing each item globally so that when it arrives, the recipient feels seen — truly, completely seen. Nothing is generic. Nothing is rushed. This is our promise.

01

Hand-Sourced Global Items

From Kyoto tea houses to Peruvian chocolate estates — every product is selected in person, never from a catalogue.

02

Bespoke Packaging

Our signature rigid boxes are crafted in Lagos, lined with heritage fabric, and sealed with wax bearing your recipient's initial.

03

White-Glove Delivery

Your gift is hand-delivered by our concierge team. No courier vans. No crumpled corners. It arrives as it was meant to arrive.

04

Personalised Storytelling

Every box ships with a handwritten card narrating the origin of each item — because context transforms a gift into a story.
